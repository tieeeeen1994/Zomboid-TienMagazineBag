# Tien's Ammo Bags

Assign any bag, pouch or holster you are already wearing as an ammo bag, then move your ammunition in and out from the firearm radial menu.

To assign one, right click a worn item that can hold things and pick "Assign Ammo Bag". There are no new items to find or craft. Assign as many as you like.

Hold your Reload key with a gun in hand for these entries:

- **Store Spent Ammo** — stows empty and part-used magazines, loose rounds and ammo boxes, cartons and crates for the gun in your hands, every gun on your hotbar, and every gun on Tien's Customizable Hotbar if you use it. Magazines on your hotbar and anything you marked as favourite stay.
- **Store All Ammo** — the same, with your full magazines as well. Only shown when you have a full magazine to store.
- **Reload All** — refills every magazine you are carrying using loose rounds from your inventory and your bags, opening ammo boxes (then cartons, then crates) only for what the loose rounds can't cover. The magazine in your gun stays in while the spares are filled, so you can stop at any moment and still fight; only at the end is it swapped for a full one and topped up itself. An empty gun gets a magazine first. It picks a magazine holding the same round you were last firing, or your biggest magazine if none do. When it is done, every loose round and every box, carton or crate of ammo your gun or its magazines take that is left in your main inventory goes into your ammo bags (anything you marked as favourite stays).
- **Fetch Fresh Ammo** — takes fresh ammunition back out, stopping before the weight would leave you encumbered.

Reloading works a magazine at a time, so the ammunition in your hands never piles up no matter how many you are filling.

Revolvers, shotguns, lever-actions and the hunting rifle are supported too, storing and fetching their loose rounds. For them Reload All loads the gun first, then opens their ammo boxes into loose rounds in your ammo bags, as many as the bags hold, so your Reload key never has to wait for a box. Entries that only make sense for magazines stay hidden.

Containers that only accept certain items are respected. Assign a shoulder holster and it takes the two pistol magazines it will hold, while everything else carries on to your next bag.

Cartons and crates Reload All opens put the boxes and cartons it did not need back in the bag they came from. If walking interrupts a box opening, the reload starts again once you stand still; running cancels it. With Tien's Skill Mastery's Running Reload, running no longer stops this mod's reloads, and a box opening cut short by running starts again too.

Each entry can be turned on or off, per player, in Settings, and each has its own key in the game's key bindings (Tien's Ammo Bags section), unbound until you set one.

In multiplayer, other players see you open ammo boxes properly instead of standing still with the box in your hand until you move. This can be turned off with the "Fix Box Opening Animation For Other Players" sandbox option, on the server's Tien's Ammo Bags page.

With a B42 gun pack built on Gunworks Gang, you also get:

- **Every magazine your gun takes** — a rifle that takes 20, 30 and 60 round magazines has all of them stored, reloaded and fetched.
- **Every kind of round** — hollow point, armor piercing, subsonic and the rest your gun accepts are stored and fetched together, whatever is loaded right now.
- **Speedloaders and stripper clips** — stored, refilled and fetched like magazines. Hold a revolver that takes them and Reload All loads the revolver first, then refills every speedloader you are carrying.
- **Taped jungle magazines** — with two 30 round magazines taped together in your gun, Reload All counts both sides, takes the pack out at the end and refills the side you emptied too. Your Reload key still flips to the other side.

To choose which round a magazine, speedloader, revolver, shotgun or lever-action is filled with, right click it and pick "Assign Ammo". Its tooltip shows your choice, and a magazine keeps it while it is in your gun. Anything assigned only ever takes its own round, whether you reload from the radial menu or with your Reload key. If you run out of that round it waits, rather than taking something else. Speedloaders without a round of their own follow the revolver in your hand. Pick "Unassign Ammo" to go back to filling it with whatever rounds you have.
Supports Project Zomboid B41 (outdated) and B42.
